Company Knowledge Base: Q3 2026

1. Executive Summary
Nexus Dynamics is a global tech firm headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company was founded in 2022 by CEO Sarah Jenkins and CTO Michael Chen. Nexus specializes in AI-driven supply chain optimization and enterprise resource planning (ERP) software.

2. Product Line
- Nexus Flow: An AI-powered logistics and routing tool. It accounts for weather, traffic, and fuel efficiency.
- Nexus Core: The foundational ERP platform used by over 500 enterprise clients.
- Nexus Vision: A newly launched computer vision system designed for warehouse inventory tracking.

3. Key Personnel
- Sarah Jenkins (Chief Executive Officer): Focuses on corporate strategy and investor relations.
- Michael Chen (Chief Technology Officer): Leads the software engineering and data science teams.
- Elena Rostova (VP of Customer Success): Manages client onboarding and support operations.

4. Company Policies
- Remote Work: All employees are allowed to work remotely, but are required to attend an annual summit in Austin every October.
- Office Hours: Core hours for team collaboration are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.
- Data Privacy: All client data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and is never shared with third-party vendors.

5. Recent Announcements
In August 2026, Nexus Dynamics acquired a smaller robotics firm, AutoBuild Solutions, to integrate physical robotic arms with the Nexus Vision inventory system. The merger made headlines and expanded the company's hardware division.
